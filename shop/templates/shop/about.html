About My Awesome Cart

India's most customer-centric company, where you can find just about anything you want to buy online

Our Story

Founded in 2010, My Awesome Cart started as a small online bookstore with a big vision. Today, we're India's largest online marketplace and most trusted brand, serving millions of customers across the country.

What makes us different is our obsessive focus on customer satisfaction. We work tirelessly to provide the best prices, fastest deliveries, and most seamless shopping experience possible.

From humble beginnings in a garage to becoming a household name, our journey has been fueled by innovation, customer trust, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

Our Mission

To be Earth's most customer-centric company, where customers can find and discover anything they might want to buy online, and endeavors to offer its customers the lowest possible prices.

Our Vision

To build the future of commerce in India by creating a platform that empowers businesses of all sizes to reach millions of customers while providing an exceptional shopping experience.
